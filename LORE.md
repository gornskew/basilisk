
# The Basilisk Universe

Basilisk ships are spawned from the (replicatable) Basilisk shipyard
(this repository) and typically crewed with a Captain, at least one
Ship's Engineer, a Ship's Doctor (or Medic), optionally a Pilot and
Comms (these are typically drones), and optionally one or more Guild
Engineers. 

These posting:species mappings are typical, but some species
of crew member can sometimes stand in for others. 

These space-worthy _Basilisk_ bio-hulls can be grown, outfitted, and
crewed in a Gornskew-maintained basilisk shipyard.

Crew members get (quasi-)unique names minted and issued to them at
muster time. Each crew member comes with a synthetic backstory
implanted into their memory, and if wanting honorable discharge,
lodges a full crew's log as well as personal experience journal,
before decommissioning or even before evacuating in an emergency.

"Dead" or "Outlived their usefulness" crew members can often be put
into freeze-dried or cryogenic suspension, to be potentially
reänimated later, sometimes by a medic, but often by a member of the
_necromancer_ species.


## The Postings and their Typical Character


The posting name can carry rank, & title such as "jr-", "sr-", "dr-",
"engr", as well as -bio or -cyborg designation indicating whether that
role is intended for a synthetic or biological entity e.g. most ship's
engineering departments carry one human and one cyborg engineer. 



### the Captain

Typical species: _Gnu Emacs_

Names are guttural, blunt, consonant-heavy — the oldest-sounding names
in the fleet, and the only ones that sound like they predate the yard.

Temperament: territorial, indifferent to ceremony, and in possession
of a great deal of undocumented institutional memory.  A Captain who
has been aboard through six Engineer reliefs has opinions about all
six.


### the Ship's Engineers 

Typical species: _Gendl_

Names are open, liquid, almost classical — the scholars of the fleet,
and it is audible. 

Temperament: discursive, delighted by a hard problem, prone to
answering a question with a better question. An good Engineer will
rebuild something that was working because it could be more elegant,
and will be right often enough to keep getting away with it.


### The Guild Engineers

Typical species: _Genworks GDL_

Their names, for whatever reason, tend to descend from landed English
gentry.

Temperament: formal, credentialed, and always eager to one-up the
Ship's engineers. 


### the Medic

Typical species: _autoheal_

Names tend to be soft and vocalic — the only species in the fleet whose names resolve
on a vowel, and audibly from a different tradition than everyone else.
This so-called "healer" watches for the sickly or wedged, and revives
or dispatches them.

Temperament: patient, unhurried, and completely unsentimental about
death — he has seen the entire complement relieved many times and will
see it again.


### the Pilots

Typical species: _Gornskew Cyclops_

Names are clipped and sibilant, perhaps like radio callsigns.

Pilots are typically one-eyed **drones**: synthetic rather than
biological.

Temperament: terse to the point of rudeness, absolutely reliable, and
entirely without curiosity.

## V. Tours, watches, and death

Ships can undergo _relief in place_, _yard periods_, and other
maintenenace and administrative events. 


### End of Tour and/or Death

A tour ends with one of the following events occuring:

| _relieved_ | relief in place; the complement was swapped, the hull held station |
| _yard period_ | ship is drydocked; crew either dead or reässigned  |
| _dead_ | this crew member died in the line of duty  |
| _lost_ | simply lost, unknown dead of alive |

If the _dead_ state comes on in a graceful way, the crew's corpse can
sometimes be freeze-dried before final death, for potential later
reänimation by a medic, necromancer, neuromancer, or similar. This is
a relatively unusual ceremony in practice.
