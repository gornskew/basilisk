# The yard's picture pouch

Settled likenesses only. Working studies, mood pieces, and the
painters' direction papers are kept off the public books until they
settle; what this pouch holds is the look to believe.

  plate the pouch holds.

yellow-eyed dirigible of a creature. See BASILISK.md, "The vat
