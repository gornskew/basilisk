# The yard's picture pouch

Everything in this pouch is concept art at this juncture — studies,
not plates. Degrees of faithfulness:

- **Top level** — ship portraits and interior studies. The long,
  yellow-eyed, dirigible-bodied look of the class in the portraits is
  on the money: treat it as the closest thing to canon appearance.
  The `grok-interior-*` studies are mood and scale, not deck plans.
- **`rough-canon/`** — vat-hall studies: staged growth down the line,
  wrights at their consoles, a float-out. The halls are close; the
  hull appearances in them still want refinement.
- **`non-canon/`** — mood only. Nothing here binds the canon.
- **`direction/`** — working papers between the yard and its
  painters; kept off the public books (see the repo `.gitignore`).
  What they settle is distilled into the canon scrolls, not read from
  here.
